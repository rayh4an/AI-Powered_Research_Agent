### Advancements in Nuclear Reactor Design

SMRs offer improved efficiency and cost-effectiveness compared to traditional nuclear power plants, with lower capital costs and shorter construction times [Just Dial, 2023]. ### Advances in Nuclear Waste Management
Advances in nuclear waste management have been made, with a focus on safe disposal and minimizing environmental impact. Deep geological repositories, advanced vitrification, and new reactor designs for recycling and transmutation are some of the innovations being explored [Net Zero Insights, 2023; WJARR, 2019; Technology Review, 2026]. These advancements aim to reduce the risks associated with nuclear waste disposal and provide a more sustainable solution for the long-term storage and disposal of nuclear waste.
